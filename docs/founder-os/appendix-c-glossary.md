# Appendix C: Glossary

> **Appendices** · [← Appendix B](appendix-b-claude-protocol.md) · [Handbook index](README.md)

Shared vocabulary, so a new hire reads this handbook without a search engine. Terms link to the chapter that owns them.

- **ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition)** — speech → timed text. Our first pipeline stage; currently OpenAI Whisper. ([Ch. 15](15-model-layer.md))
- **Assisted tier** — the default product: automated pipeline plus the customer's reviewer in our editor. ([Ch. 21](21-product-philosophy-editor.md))
- **Benchmark set** — the 30–50-clip stratified golden set with linguist references; judge of all model swaps. ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
- **Bright line** — "consented transformation, never fabrication": the product-defining trust boundary. ([Ch. 5](05-responsible-synthetic-media.md))
- **C2PA** — Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity; the standard for cryptographically signed content-origin metadata we adopt for outputs. ([Ch. 5](05-responsible-synthetic-media.md))
- **Code-switching** — mixing languages mid-sentence (ubiquitous Hindi-English in Indian lectures); a priority failure surface for ASR/MT and a target Indic-depth advantage. ([Ch. 2](02-mission-vision-category-thesis.md), [Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
- **Composite dub score** — weighted roll-up of the six quality axes; for dashboards and marketing, never for decisions (the vector decides). ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
- **Contract tests** — the suite any implementation of a stage interface must pass; what makes model swaps mechanically safe. ([Ch. 10](10-testing-quality.md))
- **Correction event** — the structured record of a human edit: job, segment, stage, before/after, action type. The moat's atomic unit. ([Ch. 20](20-data-strategy.md))
- **Correction flywheel** — corrections → better evals/triage/models → higher quality → more customers → more corrections. The primary moat. ([Ch. 4](04-moat-map.md), [Ch. 20](20-data-strategy.md))
- **CPDM (Cost Per Dubbed Minute)** — total marginal cost, review minutes and license fees included, to deliver one reviewed dubbed minute; infrastructure's governing metric. ([Ch. 11](11-infra-gpu-cost.md))
- **Decision record** — one-page written record of a hard-to-reverse decision, with an explicit revisit-condition. Lives in `docs/decisions/`. ([Ch. 3](03-values-decision-frameworks.md))
- **Decision stack** — Trust > Quality > Cost > Speed; the company's conflict-resolution order, ranked by irreversibility. ([Ch. 3](03-values-decision-frameworks.md))
- **DER (Diarization Error Rate)** — standard metric for who-spoke-when accuracy; gates any multi-speaker feature. ([Ch. 18](18-multispeaker-emotion.md))
- **Diarization** — segmenting audio by speaker identity. ([Ch. 18](18-multispeaker-emotion.md))
- **DPDP Act** — India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act; our home data-protection regime (voice prints are personal data). ([Ch. 12](12-security-compliance.md))
- **Debt register** — `docs/debt.md`: known shortcuts, what they'll break, and the observable trigger that forces repayment. ([Ch. 14](14-tech-debt.md))
- **Frontier set** — golden-set tier grown from real production correction clusters; the benchmark no competitor can replicate without our traffic. ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
- **Gauntlet** — the ten-question feature-acceptance test (nine moat/adoption questions plus the trust gate). ([Ch. 3](03-values-decision-frameworks.md))
- **Golden pipeline test** — small end-to-end fixture run asserting structural sanity (durations, non-silence, artifacts present), never byte-exactness. ([Ch. 10](10-testing-quality.md))
- **Isochrony** — translated speech occupying the source's time; the dub-vs-voiceover difference and our central research problem. ([Ch. 17](17-isochrony.md))
- **LSE-C / LSE-D** — Lip Sync Error (Confidence / Distance), SyncNet-based automatic lip-sync metrics; the harness ships in `backend/Wav2Lip_repo/evaluation/`. ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
- **Managed tier** — we supply the reviewers; bounded at 30% of revenue by written alarm (the anti-agency rule). ([Ch. 21](21-product-philosophy-editor.md))
- **Model registry** — per-model record of license, eval scores, cost, and operational notes; no entry, no deploy. ([Ch. 15](15-model-layer.md))
- **Model sovereignty** — the principle that every pipeline component is legally usable and technically replaceable behind a stage interface. ([Ch. 7](07-architecture-principles.md))
- **MOS (Mean Opinion Score)** — calibrated human panel rating (typically 1–5) for perceptual quality like voice naturalness. ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
- **MT (Machine Translation)** — the translation stage; swap target #1 (IndicTrans2 the first candidate) for both legal and quality reasons. ([Ch. 15](15-model-layer.md))
- **Post-conditions** — structural sanity checks run on every production job; violations mean `failed`, never silent delivery. ([Ch. 13](13-reliability-releases.md))
- **Review triage** — confidence-score-driven routing of segments to light vs. full human review; the mechanism that makes review cost scale sub-linearly. ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md), [Ch. 21](21-product-philosophy-editor.md))
- **Revisit-condition** — the named observable event that reopens a decision record; what keeps decisions from fossilizing. ([Ch. 3](03-values-decision-frameworks.md))
- **Shadow run** — a candidate model processing real inputs alongside production, outputs scored but never delivered; the standard pre-promotion step. ([Ch. 19](19-experimentation.md))
- **Stage interfaces** — `Transcriber`, `Translator`, `Synthesizer`, `LipSyncer` (later `Diarizer`): task-shaped contracts models are swapped behind. ([Ch. 7](07-architecture-principles.md), [Ch. 15](15-model-layer.md))
- **TTS (Text-to-Speech)** — synthesis stage; stock neural voices or consented voice cloning. ([Ch. 15](15-model-layer.md))
- **Trust dossier** — the plain-language packaging of consent/provenance/deletion commitments that procurement officers can forward; the trust moat as a sales asset. ([Ch. 23](23-enterprise-readiness.md))
- **Voice cloning** — synthesizing speech in a specific speaker's voice from a reference sample; consent-gated and premium-priced. ([Ch. 5](05-responsible-synthetic-media.md), [Ch. 24](24-pricing-unit-economics.md))
- **Wedge** — the deliberately narrow entry market: Hindi→Marathi education content. ([Ch. 2](02-mission-vision-category-thesis.md))
- **WER (Word Error Rate)** — standard ASR accuracy metric against reference transcripts. ([Ch. 16](16-evaluation-os.md))
